How to read these KPIs

TTFT and total latency are measured from the click on Run. Token count and generation speed come from the native llama.cpp timings when available. Process RSS belongs to the owned model server. On Apple Silicon, IOAccelerator GPU utilization and memory are system-wide samples—not model-exclusive VRAM claims.
